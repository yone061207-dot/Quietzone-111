PLEASE REPORT

Issues that interrupt studying

  • Loud conversations, calls or device audio in silent zones.
  • Repeated noise at a particular place or time.
  • Dining, gaming or group meetings in designated study spaces.
Start a report →
KEEP IT RESPECTFUL

Describe actions, not identities

  • Give the exact building, floor, zone and seat.
  • Avoid names, faces, student IDs and private conversations.
  • Use neutral descriptions. Do not confront others or record them secretly.

For immediate danger, contact campus security directly. QuietZone is designed for non-emergency study-space concerns.

YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before you report

Can I report anonymously?

Yes. The form does not ask for a name or email by default. If you choose follow-up, those fields become required. In a deployed service, access would need to be restricted to authorized staff. This prototype stores report details in this browser.

Do I have to upload a photo?

No. A clear location and factual description can be enough. If useful, attach one JPG or PNG up to 5 MB. Do not include identifiable people or private information. The prototype previews the photo for the current session; it does not save or send image bytes.

What do the statuses mean?

Submitted → Acknowledged → En route → On-site → Mediated → Resolved → Closed. Each stage explains what the proctor has done. “Pending information” means the reporter needs to clarify details before work can continue. Prototype samples illustrate these states; they do not advance automatically.

What if someone already reported the same issue?

The prototype checks reports created on this device for the same category and seat within 30 minutes. You can support the existing report or confirm that yours is a separate issue. A live version would need a shared database to detect duplicates across users.

What happens if more information is requested?

The tracking page shows the staff question and a reply field. Keep your reference number and check back. The proposed service would notify users who opted into follow-up; this prototype does not send emails or text messages.

Who receives the report, and how fast will they respond?

The planned recipients are student proctors and library facility coordinators. Actual response time depends on staff availability. The design shows a staff update instead of promising a response time that the system cannot guarantee.

How is my information handled in this prototype?

Reports, contact details and replies are stored in this browser’s local storage, with an in-memory fallback if storage is blocked. Nothing is sent to a server. Avoid entering real personal data on a shared device. Use the button below to remove locally created reports.

Why this page matters

Clear guidelines help students submit useful reports while reducing unnecessary photos, incomplete information and avoidable conflict.

Need help locating a seat?

Check the seat label or nearby zone sign. If you cannot identify the location, ask the library service desk before submitting.
